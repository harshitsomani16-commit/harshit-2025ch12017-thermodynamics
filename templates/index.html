CALCULATION METHOD

One generalized cubic framework. Four model families.

The solver forms a cubic polynomial in compressibility factor Z, filters physically admissible roots, selects a phase root and evaluates residual thermodynamic functions. Pure-component fugacity is calculated as f = φP.

Z³ + c₂Z² + c₁Z + c₀ = 0
01
Pure component

No mixture interaction parameters or binary interaction coefficients are used.

02
Ideal-gas baseline

H and S integrate the temperature-dependent ideal-gas Cp/R polynomial from Appendix C, Table C.1, plus EOS residual terms.

03
Model limitations

Cubic EOS estimates can be less reliable for strongly polar/associating fluids and near critical states. Appendix C Cp fits are stated for 298 K to Tmax.
